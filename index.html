Origin, Insertion & Contraction Phases

Origin (Fixed)

Fixed proximal bone attachment that does not move during contraction.

Insertion (Moving)

Distal attachment that moves closer to the origin when contracting.

Concentric ("Positive")

Shortening/lifting phase as the insertion pulls toward the origin.

Eccentric ("Negative")

Controlled lengthening/lowering phase—key driver of hypertrophy.

Pages 8–11, 60–61 • Progression & RPE

Wave Loading, Double Progression & After Week 9

  • • 3-Week Wave Loading: Weeks 1, 4, and 7 are "mini-deloads" (RPE 7–8). Weeks 2, 5, 8 build effort, and Weeks 3, 6, 9 peak (RPE 9–10).
  • • Double Progression (+2.5% 1RM): Primary lifts add 1 rep per week (e.g., 4 → 5 → 6 reps), then increase load by ~2.5% 1RM at the start of the next wave.
  • • RPE / RIR Scale: RPE 10 = Failure (0 reps left) • RPE 9 = 1 rep left • RPE 8 = 2 reps left • RPE 7 = 3 reps left.
  • • What To Do After Week 9: Either repeat the 9-week block from Week 1 with +2.5% updated 1RMs, or transition into Jeff Nippard’s Push/Pull/Legs Hypertrophy Program.
Pages 62–73 • Execution, Volume & Nutrition

Form Cues, Weekly Volume & Recovery Hints

  • • Controlling the Negative: Aim for a controlled 1–2s eccentric and 1–2s concentric on secondary/tertiary lifts; explosive concentric on primary lifts.
  • • Breathing & Valsalva: Inhale during the eccentric (lowering) and exhale during the concentric (lifting). Use the Valsalva maneuver cautiously on heavy compounds.
  • • Mind-Muscle Connection (MMC): Focus externally on whole-body movement during heavy squats/bench/deadlifts; use internal muscle squeezing on isolations.
  • • Weekly Volume & Diet: 10–20 sets per muscle/week is optimal. Eat in a slight caloric surplus with 0.8–1.0 g/lb protein; keep cardio to 1–4 low-intensity 20–30m sessions if cutting.
